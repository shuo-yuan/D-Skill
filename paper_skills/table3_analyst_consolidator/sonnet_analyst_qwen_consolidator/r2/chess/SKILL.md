## When NOT to Analyze: Fast Paths and Stopping Rules

settles the position, when further verification cannot change the
decision, and when a line of analysis should be abandoned. Every entry
must name (a) the exact trigger that makes the shortcut safe and
(b) the condition under which the shortcut must NOT be taken. A rule
without both parts does not belong here.

Condition to skip: (a) every adjacent king square is covered or blocked, and (b) no opponent piece can capture or interpose. If verified, commit immediately with exactly one `<action>` tag. Condition to NOT skip: king has an escape square, the capturing piece is undefended, or verification fails.

## Verifying the Move: Tool Ritual

- **Simulate Forcing Replies**: If a threat/safety result identifies a forcing opponent reply, run `<simulate>` on the candidate followed by that reply. Trace forcing checks/replies explicitly.

- **Decisive/Flagged Moves (`#`, `+`)**: Perform exactly one quick visual pass (check king escapes and piece protection). If it checks out, commit immediately or issue exactly one confirming tool call. Do not re-litigate square occupancy.
- **Promotion Races**: Never assume victory based on move count alone. Use `<line>` or `<simulate>` to verify the push cannot be stopped by a check-then-capture sequence or other forcing defense.
