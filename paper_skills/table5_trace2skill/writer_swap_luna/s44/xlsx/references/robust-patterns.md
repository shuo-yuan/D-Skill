## Robust Patterns\n- **Relative-date cutoff:** Scan the target date column, find the max date, subtract the interval, and use that threshold. This makes filtering independent of the execution date.\n- **Formatting preservation:** When transferring cell data, copy `cell.value`, `cell.number_format`, and `cell.font`/`style` metadata to the destination.\n- **Worksheet by position:** Use `workbook.worksheets[n-1]` (0-indexed) when a sheet is specified by its 1-based number. Apply structural changes (column delete, rename, move) to that specific sheet object.
